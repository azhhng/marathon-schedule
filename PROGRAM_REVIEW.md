# Program consistency review — October 25, 2026

## Result

All 51 weeks were audited against the current agreed program. Calendar boundaries, run counts, scheduled distances, checkpoints, optional pace work, taper, and fueling arithmetic are consistent. The running distances were not increased or reduced during this review.

This is an internal consistency review, not a prediction of an individual's readiness or an assurance that the future peak workload will be suitable.

## Corrections

- **Race week:** the display and chart now distinguish 9 km of training from the 42.195 km race. The underlying combined distance remains 51.195 km. There are three short easy sessions plus the marathon; Friday's 2 km is explicitly an optional shakeout.
- **Strength:** removed strength suggestions from Monday/Saturday recovery cards. Start one optional session after Tuesday from Week 2 only if the first runs feel comfortable. Thursday becomes an optional second session from Week 4. Keep strength light in the taper and omit it in race week. Exercise examples and repetitions are expandable within sessions.
- **Warm-up and units:** first ten weeks use total session minutes, including two 5-minute walks. Later kilometre targets cover the main run/walk section, including walking breaks; add the warm-up and cool-down walks. The log records actual total distance/time including those walks. Race day follows the rehearsed race routine.
- **Fueling:** early guidance explicitly means no *mid-run* carbohydrate requirement for comfortable short sessions with normal meals, not avoiding carbs in meals. Weekend long-run breakfast guidance is separate from after-work snacks. Five selected Sunday sessions have fueling-rehearsal prompts linked to the Fueling tab.
- **Documentation and publishing:** corrected stale local documentation, synchronized local preview files with `docs`, and added content-based versions for every browser asset to avoid showing older instructions.

## Current structure

- Start: October 26, 2026. Race: October 17, 2027, 8 AM, using the user's supplied race details.
- Three runs weekly initially; four from February 1 if ready; five from June 28 if ready.
- Build toward a repeatable 38–40 km four-day base before the five-day block. This may require repeating weeks rather than following dates automatically.
- Five-day pattern: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday. Monday/Friday do not contain runs until the race-week Friday shakeout.
- May 9: 10K checkpoint. July 11: controlled half-marathon checkpoint. Each replaces Sunday's run.
- Optional Wednesday pace sessions: July 21, August 11, September 15. Each is within the existing distance in a cutback week and may be replaced by entirely easy running.
- Peak: September 20–26, 70 km across 9 + 14 + 8 + 4 + 35 km. Longest run is Sunday, September 26.
- Taper training totals: 46 km, 33 km, then 9 km before race day.

## Decisions still dependent on real training

- **70 km and 35 km are provisional.** At 7:30/km, 35 km is about 4 hours 23 minutes; time on feet, recovery, and available time must be reviewed before attempting it. A shorter long run or lower-volume week remains valid.
- A controlled half marathon helps assess endurance, fueling, and recovery; it is not a stand-alone predictor of a sub-five-hour marathon.
- Fuel and sodium needs remain individual. Calculator portions are label-based examples, and carbohydrate in drinks counts toward the total. No fixed fluid/sodium dose is imposed.
- The discussion of body weight does not add a weight-loss requirement, calorie prescription, or public personal measurements to the program.
